FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The coffee questions we hear most often at the roastery.

How fresh is your coffee?

Every bag is roasted to order and shipped within one working day of the roast. The roast date is printed on every bag — look for it near the bottom. We recommend brewing filter coffees between day 5 and day 21 after roast, when aromatics peak.

How should I store coffee beans?

Keep the beans in their original sealed bag (or an airtight container) at room temperature, away from light and moisture. Don't refrigerate — coffee absorbs fridge odours and condensation stales it faster. Buy the amount you'll finish in two to three weeks, and grind just before brewing.

Which coffee should a beginner try?

Start with Araku Valley — Medium Roast. It's balanced, forgiving across brewing methods, and sweet with milk or without. If you prefer your coffee bright and black, step up to Chikmagalur Estate — Light Roast once you're comfortable.

Can I use your coffee in an espresso machine?

Yes — two of our coffees are built for it. Bengaluru Dark (Dark Roast) and Coorg Highlands (Medium-Dark) both pull well as espresso and stand up to milk. Lighter filter roasts can be pulled as espresso too, but they'll taste brighter and take grind adjustment — our Brewing page has the full starting recipe.

How long should coffee beans rest after roasting?

Freshly roasted coffee vents CO₂ for a few days, which makes early brews uneven. Rest filter roasts 4–7 days; rest dark espresso roasts 7–10 days for the most stable shots. After roughly three weeks, drink up — aroma slowly fades from there.
