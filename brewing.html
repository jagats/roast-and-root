Brewing Guide

Brew It Like You Mean It

Five reliable recipes from our roastery bench — plus a calculator that scales any of them to your cup.

Method 01

Pour Over

Pour over rewards patience with the cleanest, most aromatic cup. Best with our light roasts — Chikmagalur Estate or the Nilgiri lots.

ParameterRecommendation
Coffee18 g (for one ~300 ml cup)
Water300 ml at 93°C
Grind sizeMedium-fine, like coarse sand
Brew time3 – 3.5 minutes

Method

  1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and discard the rinse water.
  2. Add 18 g of coffee, level the bed, and tare your scale to zero.
  3. Bloom: pour 50 ml of water in a spiral, swirl gently, and wait 30–40 seconds.
  4. Pour the remaining water in slow, steady spirals to 300 ml, keeping the bed level.
  5. Let it drain fully (target 3–3.5 minutes), swirl the cup, and enjoy.
Method 02

French Press

The most forgiving brewer there is — full immersion means full body and rich texture. Try it with Araku Valley Medium or Coorg Highlands.

ParameterRecommendation
Coffee30 g (for two cups, 1:15 ratio)
Water450 ml at 95°C
Grind sizeCoarse, like sea salt
Brew time4 minutes

Method

  1. Add coffee and all the water at once; stir gently to wet every ground.
  2. Rest 4 minutes with the lid on, plunger up.
  3. Break the crust with a spoon and skim the floating foam.
  4. Wait 3 more minutes, then press slowly — stopping at the surface of the coffee, not to the bottom.
  5. Pour immediately so it doesn't keep brewing in the beaker.
Method 03

AeroPress

Quick, portable and endlessly tweakable — the AeroPress is the roastery's favourite travel companion. Great with Coorg Sun-Dried.

ParameterRecommendation
Coffee15 g
Water220 ml at 90°C
Grind sizeMedium, like table salt
Brew timeAbout 2 minutes

Method

  1. Set up the AeroPress inverted, add coffee and 220 ml of water, stir 3 times.
  2. Brew for 1 minute 30 seconds.
  3. Cap with the rinsed filter, flip carefully onto your cup, and press gently for 30 seconds.
  4. Stop at the hiss. Top up with a splash of hot water if you like a lighter cup.
Method 04

Espresso

Espresso is a controlled recipe, not a mystery. Start here and adjust one variable at a time. Use Bengaluru Dark or Coorg Highlands.

ParameterRecommendation
Coffee18 g, finely ground (like powdered sugar)
Yield36 g of espresso out
Temperature92–94°C
Brew time25 – 30 seconds

Method

  1. Dose 18 g into the basket and distribute the grounds evenly.
  2. Tamp level and firm — consistency matters more than force.
  3. Lock in and start the shot immediately; time and weigh it.
  4. Too fast (under 25 s)? Grind finer. Too slow (over 30 s)? Grind coarser.
  5. Taste. If it's sour, grind finer; if it's bitter and dry, grind coarser.
Method 05

Cold Brew

Slow, sweet and smooth — cold brew trades acidity for chocolatey roundness. Chikmagalur Estate makes a beautiful batch.

ParameterRecommendation
Coffee100 g, coarse grind
Water800 ml, cold or room temperature (1:8)
Steep time14 – 18 hours in the fridge
YieldAbout 600 ml of concentrate — dilute 1:1 with water or milk

Method

  1. Combine coarse-ground coffee and water in a large jar; stir to wet everything.
  2. Cover and steep in the fridge for 14–18 hours.
  3. Strain through a paper filter or fine cloth — twice for a cleaner cup.
  4. Dilute 1:1 to serve, and keep refrigerated for up to a week.
